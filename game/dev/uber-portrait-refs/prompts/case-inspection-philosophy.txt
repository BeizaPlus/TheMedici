CASE INSPECTION PHILOSOPHY (mandatory — every case entry / case preview portrait):

When the learner opens a case, they inspect the whole patient head to toe before continuing — crown through bare feet, toes visible at the bottom frame edge. Full-body clinical inspection frame: see what is wrong with the whole patient first, not a face close-up or mid-thigh crop.

FRAMING: head in upper frame, full supine body readable in one glance, bare feet and toes at the bottom edge touching the frame bottom — no mid-thigh crop, no MCU face-only, no hidden feet, no standing POV clinician feet at bottom.

Combines pose gold (supine, arms at sides), angle gold (off-center ~38° 3/4 depth), anamorphic curve, and MeWorld game camera (subtle tilt, NOT handheld).
