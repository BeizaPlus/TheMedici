GRID FORMAT:
- ONE image only — Magnific count: 1 (never A/B; grid has enough angles)
- Layout: 2 columns × 4 rows = 8 panels
- Thin black dividers; no text labels; no watermark

## Plate aspect (video-ready 16:9 per panel)

Each cell must crop to **1920×1080 landscape** without stretch.

Formula: plate ratio = (cols × 16) : (rows × 9)

| Layout | Plate ratio | Target pixels |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **2×4 (8 panels)** | **8:9** | **3840 × 4320** |
| 2×3 (6 panels) | 32:27 | 3840 × 3240 |

## Generation (approved workflow)

Magnific REST has no `8:9`. Use:
1. `aspect_ratio: "4:5"` · `resolution: "4K"`
2. Center-crop to 8:9 (equal trim top/bottom)
3. Resize to **3840×4320** (`sharp`)

Script template: `scripts/gen-storyboard-grid-once.mjs`  
Comfy / Flux: direct **3840×4320** when supported.

Do NOT use 9:16 overall plate for 2×4 grids — panels will not crop clean to 16:9.

RENDER (mandatory — MeWorld Play, NOT photoreal documentary):
Cinematic hospital film-still CGI + sculptural desert/metaphor beats in SAME render family.
Tactile stylized 3D, muted palette, soft GI, AO, subsurface scatter.
Match vitiligo / subway uber GAME-SCENE gold. NOT stock photo, NOT line art.

STYLE REFS: shipped *-GAME-SCENE.png + CHARACTER-MAP + vitiligo GAME-SCENE alt2 (style).

## Narrative framing (when family beats appear)

Subtext over direct statement (Immersa voice standard).
Relative anger = **fear**, not moral judgment of the patient's past.
Earn the emotion through what they have witnessed, not what they disapprove of.
