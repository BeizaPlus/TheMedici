ANATOMY COMPOSITION LOCK (mandatory — invoke on every patient portrait and pediatric baseplate gen):

Patient lies SUPINE on the mattress — head toward the top of frame, feet toward the bottom of frame.
Legs extend along the bed lengthwise (parallel to mattress long axis) — natural resting bend at knees — bare feet at the foot of the bed pointing toward the foot rail, NOT toward the camera, NOT vertical like standing.
Arms rest at sides on the sheet. Torso flat on mattress. No standing pose, no seated pose, no legs raised toward overhead camera.

CAMERA: high bedside view from foot of bed toward head (~38° from vertical) — NOT bird's-eye 90° direct overhead. Match reference scene lock exactly. Third-person mounted game camera — NOT first-person standing at foot of bed.

FORBIDDEN: examiner/clinician POV feet at frame bottom; second person's feet; standing-over-patient camera. ALLOWED: patient's own toes on mattress at bottom edge.
