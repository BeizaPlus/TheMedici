# Published log

Log every post after it goes live. Keeps us from repeating and lets us read what works.

---

## How to use

- Add a row the day a post goes live. Move its post folder into `published/`.
- Drop the LinkedIn URL in Link.
- After a few days, come back and add likes / comments / standout replies in Notes. The `social-media-analyzer` skill can read these into engagement rates when there is enough data.
- Use this to keep the pillar mix honest over the year.
