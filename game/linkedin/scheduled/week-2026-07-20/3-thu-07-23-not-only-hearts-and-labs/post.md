Medicine is not only hearts and labs.

Some of the hardest cases are not solved by the right order set. **They are carried by whether you listened first.**

So we built psychiatry and social cases too. The human ones. The conversations training tends to skip, because they do not fit on a flashcard.

The simulated patient stays fully in character. It will let you rush. It will let you talk past the person in front of you and miss what they actually came in for.

If we only train for the clean cases, we leave people unprepared for the human ones. **So we built for both.**
