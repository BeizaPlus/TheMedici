A 13 year old and a physician can walk through the same case. Both should learn something.

That is my test for whether the model is right.

If the only way to learn is to already have the vocabulary, it is not teaching. **It is gatekeeping.**

A real case does not require you to know the answer first. It lets you ask, try, be wrong, and understand. The attending meets you where you are, one line or the whole mechanism.

Medicine is not a secret language. **It is a system you can understand.** Anyone willing to walk the case should be able to.
