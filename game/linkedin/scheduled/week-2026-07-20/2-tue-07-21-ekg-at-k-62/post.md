What does the heart look like at a potassium of 6.2?

Potassium sets the resting voltage of every heart cell. Push it up and the cells get twitchy, then sluggish, in a sequence you can read on the ECG.

First the **T waves go tall and peaked**, like tents. The cell repolarizes too fast.

Climb higher and the **P wave flattens and the PR stretches**. The atria struggle to fire.

Higher still and the **QRS widens**, smearing into the T, until the tracing turns into a slow sine wave. That is the heart about to stop.

The number on the chart is a warning. The ECG tells you how much time you have.
