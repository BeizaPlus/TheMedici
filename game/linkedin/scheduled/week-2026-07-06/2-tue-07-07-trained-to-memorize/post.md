I trained in a system that rewards memorizing over understanding.

You can recite the clotting cascade and still not see why this patient is bleeding. You can ace the renal block and still freeze when the potassium climbs.

**Memorization gives you facts. It does not give you a way to think when the facts do not line up.**

I spent years optimizing for the exam. The exam was never the patient.

So I stopped trying to fix the studying, and started rebuilding the training itself.
