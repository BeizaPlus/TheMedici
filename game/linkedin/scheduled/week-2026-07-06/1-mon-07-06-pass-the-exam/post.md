You can pass every exam and still freeze on your first real shift.

I know the feeling. Years of physiology, pharmacology, and pathways. Strong on paper. Then a real patient is in front of you, waiting for an answer, and your mind goes quiet.

The problem was never the knowledge. It was that I had never been in the room. **An exam rewards recall. A patient needs judgment.** Those are not the same skill, and we only train one of them.

We graduate people who can name the mechanism but cannot move when it matters.

That gap is where I started building.
