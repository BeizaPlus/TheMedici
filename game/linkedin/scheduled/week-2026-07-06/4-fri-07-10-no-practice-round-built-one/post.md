There is no practice round in medicine. So I am building one.

Every other field lets you rehearse. Pilots have simulators. Athletes scrimmage. Doctors get handed a real patient and a real consequence on day one.

That is why the first shift feels like falling. **Not a knowledge gap. A reps gap.**

So I built the room. A real case, a patient who responds to what you do, a clock that does not wait, and an attending who teaches while it happens. You can be wrong here, learn why, and try again.

**The first time you face it should not be the first time you have seen it.**
