I am documenting how I think, not just what I build.

Anyone can inherit a product. Few inherit the reasoning behind it.

So I am writing down the why under every decision. Why cases instead of flashcards. Why the physiology is fixed and the teaching is flexible. Why the patient is allowed to get worse.

**Money is a number you hand over. A blueprint is a way of thinking you pass on.**

I want my sons to inherit the second one. The thing that makes the first one repeatable.

You do not leave a legacy by hoarding the answer. **You leave it by documenting the method.**
