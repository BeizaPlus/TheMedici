Deterministic core. Narrative shell.

The most important rule in the engine. **The AI never decides whether the potassium rose.**

The physiology decides. Vitals, electrolytes, the ECG stage, all follow explicit rules written into the case. Give the wrong drug and the numbers move the way biology says they must.

The AI's job is to explain, not to invent. It reads what the engine produced and teaches the mechanism behind it.

That line matters. It is the difference between a tutor you can trust and a chatbot that will confidently make something up. **The story can be flexible. The medicine cannot.**
