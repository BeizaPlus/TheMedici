Build it. Document it. Hand it off.

That is the only way anything scales without me standing in every room.

Early on you are the system. Every decision routes through you, and it feels productive. **It is also a ceiling.**

The work that compounds is not the next feature. It is writing the thing down clearly enough that someone else can run it, and then letting them.

I am playing a nine year game, not a nine week one. Things that compound look slow until they do not.

**The founder who cannot leave the room has not built a company. He has built a job.**
