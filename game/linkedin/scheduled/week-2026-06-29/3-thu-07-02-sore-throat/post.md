Picture this: it's a busy Friday afternoon in the ER, and a patient comes in with a sore throat. In that moment, this is your emergency. But as any doctor knows, **the complaint is almost never the real diagnosis**. A sore throat could be something simple, like a virus, or it could be **the first sign that a patient's airway is about to close**.

In Immersa, you step into situations like this with all the tools you would have in real life. You get **the patient, the clock, and the room**. You ask questions, examine the patient, place your orders, and begin treatment. At the same time, an experienced attending is there with you, guiding you and explaining the underlying mechanisms as the case unfolds.

You have **twenty minutes** to work through the case, and the timer does not care how confident you feel because it just keeps ticking. Just like in real medicine, **the answer is not written at the top of the chart**. You have to find your way, make decisions, and learn as you go. This is what makes this training so valuable. It gives you a safe space to practice dealing with uncertainty and complexity, so you are ready when it matters most.
