Medicine is not only hearts and labs.

MeWorld has cases in psychiatry and social medicine too. The hard human ones. The conversations training tends to skip, because they do not fit on a flashcard.

Some cases are not solved by the right order set. They are carried by whether you listened first.

The simulated patient stays fully in character. It will let you rush, and it will let you miss the person in front of you.

If we only train for the clean cases, we leave people unprepared for the human ones.

So we built for both.
