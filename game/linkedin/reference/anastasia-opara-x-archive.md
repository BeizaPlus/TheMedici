## SHORT REPLIES — The Interaction Voice

These are Ana's casual replies to fans, colleagues, and press. They're small, but they reveal the texture of her voice in conversation — how she receives praise, how she reacts to others' work, how she signs off. This is the voice behind the threads.

| Context | Her Reply |
|---|---|
| Congratulating someone | "Congrats!! ;3" |
| Reacting to something impressive | "Wow! 😮" |
| Moved by something beautiful | "It's wonderful 🥹" |
| Simple thanks | "Thank you 😃" |
| Slightly more formal thanks | "Thank you!" |
| Warm thanks with name tag | "aww thank you @Friedemann_A :3" |
| Breezy thanks | "thank you! ^^" |
| Big grin thanks | "Thank you :D" |

**Voice notes:**
- Every reply has either an emoji or a kaomoji (`;3`, `^^`, `:3`, `:D`). Never bare text.
- The kaomoji choice is deliberate: `;3` is playful, `^^` is cheerful/demure, `:3` is cat-like warmth. These are not random — they're a second emotional channel.
- When she's moved ("It's wonderful 🥹"), the emoji does the emotional work the sentence doesn't spell out.
- Her thanks come in at least 5 different flavors — she modulates based on closeness and context.
- Nobody thanks this precisely by accident. She's thinking about how every interaction lands.

### THREAD: Undo-Redo System (May 28, 2022) — 10 tweets

**Tweet 1:**
This month I finally convinced myself to tackle the undo-redo system since it was becoming a premonition of a technical debt👀 And of course I decided to make it animated. How hard can it be, right!? 😂 [1/10] #screenshotsaturday #indiegamedev #rustlang🦀 #procgen #bevyengine

**Tweet 2:**
[2/10] Undo is essential for this game to stay playful and relaxing: if you misclick, a cat jumps on the keyboard, or you simply change your mind, it should always be easy to go back. I had been scheming an undo system for a while, and now was the time to roll up my sleeves 💪

**Tweet 3:**
[3/10] Undo-redo for terrain and paths was pretty straightforward. Since the interactions between systems are procedural, a lot of animation is happening for free 😎 For ex, arches appearing where paths cross walls, stones arranging along a path, or walls sticking to the ground

**Tweet 4:**
[4/10] The clip above is undoing-redoing a single terrain edit, and the one below is undoing-redoing a single path stroke.

**Tweet 5:**
[5/10] Undo for walls, however, turned into one of the most intricate bits of software engineering I've ever had to do 😅 A single eraser stroke can have many possible outcomes - a wall is split multiple times, erased from different sides, deleted, or any combination of those.

**Tweet 6:**
[6/10] I ended up writing a compiler for these events into a smaller number of lower-level operations that can be sanely maintained and replayed 🤓 Now, if you split a wall when erasing, undo will play the expected merging animation 🙌 No new specific case handling required!

**Tweet 7:**
[7/10] Originally, I wasn't even planning to add animations 😮 This month was only about undo-redo. But mid-way through, I realized that if I "just" added animations, I could extend undo-redo into a replay system, letting you watch your build recreated from scratch 🤩

**Tweet 8:**
[8/10] Being a huge fan of time lapses made by the Sims community, and the time lapse feature in Procreate, I felt that it would resonate with the vibe of my game ✨ Et voilà! I can now replay this small "Thank you" scene, hehe 😊

**Tweet 9:**
[9/10] Aaand that's all for the May update~ thank you for reading! And huge big mountainous thank you @h3r2tic for feedback, brainstorms and being my editor for this post 🤗🙇‍♀️💖

### The Complete Train of Thought (Emotional Arc)

Every substantial Ana post follows the same five-beat emotional journey. It's a story, not an announcement:

| Beat | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Personal energy first | Ground the post in human experience, not feature list | "Tom and I have been in a total goblin mode for the past three weeks" |
| 2. Celebrate the win excitedly | Pure joy, emoji-dense | "omg overall it really turned out better than we hoped for 💚" |
| 3. Acknowledge imperfections honestly | Builds trust. She's not selling, she's showing. | "There're still some kinks to iron out" / "it kinda got neglected" |
| 4. Emotional payoff | The struggle was worth it. Pride restored. | "my pride has been restored now 😤" / "it's starting to have a character of its own 🥰" |
| 5. Gentle invitation | Never a demand. Always a door left open. | "consider wishlisting the game 🏰" / "Thank you for reading 💚" |

This is the same arc as her individual tweets, compressed, or her threads, expanded. She never opens with the feature. She never closes with the price. The human story carries the product, not the other way around.

1. **Joy anchors** — "I've added trees and flowers! Suddenly it feels real."

## SIGGRAPH TALK QUOTE (Aug 2017) — "Believability in Procedural Modelling"

This quote ran in the 80 Level coverage of her SIGGRAPH talk. One paragraph, carrying her whole philosophy:

> Humans, are great pattern recognition machines, who face astonishing amount of complexity on a daily basis. We are so good at dealing with it, that we do not directly register how exactly this happens, yet when something is off, we immediately 'feel' it. Procedural modelling is, in a way, like painting, where complexity emerges from the interaction of simple rules. Thus, we can achieve great intricacy and believability from layering the simplest rules if we analyse correctly our own thought patterns.

**Voice notes:**
- "We are great pattern recognition machines" — this exact phrase appears in her 2016 interview and resurfaces everywhere. It's her core belief. She never stopped saying it.
- "When something is off, we immediately 'feel' it" — this is the design principle behind every procedural system she's ever built. It's also the principle behind every piece of content she's ever written.
- "Procedural modelling is, in a way, like painting" — the painting metaphor she'll later compress to "rendering is like painting 60 times per second." Same idea, different density.
