Medicine taught me to memorize. It rarely let me see.

In MeWorld the patient responds to what you actually did.

Order the right things and the numbers settle. The monitor calms. The patient looks better, and you can watch it happen. Get it wrong and they slide the other way, on purpose, so you meet the mistake here instead of on a real person.

Nobody dies in the sim. But we can simulate the crash, so the first time you face it is not the first time you have seen it.

This is medicine you practice, not medicine you recite. Personalized, immersive, and finally practical.

It is the thing I wished I had in medical school.
