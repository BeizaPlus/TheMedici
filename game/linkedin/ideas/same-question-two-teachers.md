Same question. Two teachers. Watch the answer change.

I asked my AI attending the same thing twice about the same patient: why is she hypotensive.

First answer, four sentences. She is vasodilated, the toxin dropped her vascular tone, that is why you give fluids before pressors. Tight. The way an attending answers when you are mid-resuscitation and do not have time.

Second answer, same patient, I just turned the teaching dial up. Now it walks the mechanism. The arterial wall as a hose with the nozzle wide open. Pressure bleeding off into the capillary beds. Hypotension without hypovolemia. Then it turns it back on me: what is your next move here.

Same case. Same numbers. Different teacher, because I chose how deep I wanted to go.

That is the part most med ed tools miss. A good attending reads the room. Sometimes you need the one line. Sometimes you need the whole arc. You should not have to relearn the tool to get either.

This is MeWorld. You understand the body. You do not memorize it.
