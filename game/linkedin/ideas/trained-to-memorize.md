I trained in a system that rewards memorizing over understanding.

You can pass every exam and still not see why the potassium rose.

I did that. Most of us did.

So I am building the opposite.

Real cases. A patient who gets worse when you are wrong. An attending that explains the mechanism, not the mnemonic.

The test for the model is simple. If a 13 year old and a physician can both walk the same case and both learn something, it works.

That is what MeWorld is for.
