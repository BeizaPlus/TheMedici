At rest (~0.3 ms on this chart), leak K⁺ leaves the cell until electrical pull balances chemical pull — that steady voltage is resting membrane potential, about −75 mV inside negative.

An action potential is a millisecond-scale voltage excursion. The gray trace is Vm; blue/orange are relative permeability for timing.
