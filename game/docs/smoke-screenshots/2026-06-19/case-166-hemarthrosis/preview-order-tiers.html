Case 166 · Hemophilia A (Factor VIII Deficiency)

Patient: Marcus · Slug: hemarthrosis-athlete-knee

Critical — hemophilia A hemarthrosis
Factor replacement and bleeding precautions are non-negotiable. Coags in Misc; exam + RICE in General.

Critical Case-specific orders — treatments, consults, must-not-miss

  • Factor VIII assay
    Low FVIII activity confirms hemophilia A and guides severity.
  • Factor VIII concentrate
    Definitive hemostasis for active hemarthrosis — replace what the joint cannot clot without.
  • DDAVP (mild hemophilia A)
    Mild disease may respond to desmopressin releasing stored VIII and vWF — not enough alone for severe bleed.
  • Avoid NSAIDs
    Antiplatelet effect worsens bleeding — acetaminophen only for pain.
  • Avoid IM injections
    Intramuscular hematoma risk — IV and PO routes only.

General Physical exams and core workflow

  • Physical exam: extremities / joints
    Document swollen painful knee, range of motion, and prior hemarthrosis sites — exam drives urgency of factor.
  • Supportive care (RICE, joint immobilization)
    Rest, ice, compression, elevation, and splint while factor works — protects cartilage from repeat bleed.

Misc Labs, imaging, and diagnostic tests

  • PT/PTT / Platelets
    PTT prolonged, PT normal, Plt normal — isolated intrinsic pathway defect before you commit to factor replacement.
  • Mixing study
    PTT corrects — excludes inhibitor; if it fails to correct, think acquired inhibitor not classic hemophilia A.
