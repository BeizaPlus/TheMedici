Village party

N'Gavu was out with friends from the village — beer all night, yellow jacket, nothing felt wrong. He drank like he always does after work.

N'Gavu in mustard-yellow party jacket with brown beer bottle, night party or pool hall — same face, NOT hospital gown

2

Disruption

The next afternoon, walking home in the sun, his hands and face began to peel and blister. By evening he could not stand light on his forearms.

Same N'Gavu squinting in afternoon sun, yellow jacket, blistering forearms starting — outdoor village path

3

Embodiment

In the ED he winces when the triage window lights his arms. He took off the yellow shirt for the gown — it hangs on the chair. His beer bottles are on the side table; he says they are his property.

N'Gavu supine in hospital gown on stretcher, mustard-yellow shirt on bedside chair, two beer bottles on overbed table, shields blistered forearms from window light

4

Escalation

Fragile bullae on sun-exposed skin, milia on the hands, darker patches and coarse hair on the dorsal hands — the pattern whispers porphyrin, not a simple sunburn.

Close on N'Gavu's blistered dorsal hands and forearms, same face visible, ED bay, monitor upper-right

5

Crisis point

If porphyrins keep building while orders wait, every photon through the glass keeps injuring skin — and alcohol may still be driving the trigger until you prove otherwise.

N'Gavu tense on stretcher, eyes toward bright window, beer bottles still on side table, clinical stress

6

Recontextualization

The peeling was never just sun — porphyrins in skin, alcohol as trigger, light as the weapon. Name it and the pathway opens.

N'Gavu calmer in dimmed bay, yellow shirt still on chair, forearms settling, relief mixed with shame — wide bedside depth
