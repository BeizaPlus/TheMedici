## Why these three

They present the same at the bedside: dyspnea, crackles, hypoxia. The fork is mechanism — not the first listen.

| Fork | Clues |
|------|--------|
| **Cardiomyopathy / CHF** | Orthopnea, PND, S3, JVD, edema; cardiomegaly on CXR; ↓ EF on echo |
| **Pneumonia** | Fever, pleurisy, focal consolidation; antibiotics |
| **Pulmonary edema** | Acute hydrostatic fluid; BNP ↑; diuresis; often flash decompensation of failing heart |

Pulmonary edema is frequently the acute face of cardiomyopathy. Pneumonia can trigger decompensation in a weak heart.
