minutes then gradually wakes up but is confused (part of a postictal state). Basic diagnostic workup of a first-time seizure in an adult includes chemistries and electrolytes (including blood glucose), magnesium, blood count,

medication. Starting anti-epileptic medication after a first-time, unprovoked seizure in an adult patient is only indicated if any of the following risk factors are present: EEG abnormalities, symptomatic cause based on history
‘or neuroimaging (.e. brain tumor or prior CNS infection), abnormal neurologic examination, or seizure that occurs during sleep. This patient does not meet any of these criteria, so he can be sent home with outpatient follow-
up in a few weeks. The patient should be counseled not to drive for several months, in case he has a subsequent seizure while driving.
