Case Summary

This patient has cauda equina syndrome. Cauda equina is caused by entrapment of the conus medullaris, the most distal portion of the spinal cord. It typically presents as low back pain that radiates down the leg with
perianal and perigenital numbness and/or loss of bowel and bladder function. Cauda equina syndrome is a medical emergency requiring prompt surgical intervention. Patients suspected of having cauda equina should
receive a STAT MRI and neurosurgery consult for spinal decompression. The key to this case is a fast diagnosis with a STAT MRI and a fast consult to neurosurgery. The case will end after neurosurgery sees the patient.
