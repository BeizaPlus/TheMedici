The pertinent labs to order would be @ CMP to look at liver enzymes and electrolytes, a CBC to look at hematocrit, a lipid panel to look at lipid status, and an A1c to check glucose control.

ordered. The patient should be recommended a low-calorie diet as this will be required even after the bariatric surgery.
