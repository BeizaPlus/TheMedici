Case Summary

Differential diagnosis: Substance induced psychotic disorder, Schizophrenia, Psychosis secondary to a medical condition, Delirium, Epileptic seizures, Electrolyte disturbance

This case is similar to the Schizophrenia case, but this case focuses just on correctly treating acute agitation which is a common occurrence in most emergency departments. As the patient has already assaulted a staff
member, IM medication is needed and oral medication does not need to be offered. A typical regimen given is colloquially called the 5-2-1 meaning 5mg of IM Haldol (Haloperidol), 2mg of IM Ativan (Lorazepam), and 1mg
‘of IM Cogentin. As Cogentin IM is very expensive, an alternative is a 5-2-50 which is Smg of IM Haldol, 2mg of IM Ativan (Lorazepam), and 50mg of IM Benadryl (Diphenhydramine). Droperidol and Midazolam can be used
instead of Haldol (it has a shorter half-life than Haldol) and Lorazepam (Lorazepam), but this is not as commonly used. Droperidol has an IM injection, but the USMLE only lists intravenous as an available option, so this is not
the best choice. Once the patient is treated, he will calm down and then labs and a physical exam can be completed. There can be medical causes of acute agitation which should be ruled out, but given the age of the patient

and his presentation, a primary psychiatric diagnosis is likely. Without a history, the exact diagnosis will not be determined in this case.
Other second-generation antipsychotics are available for the treatment of acute agitation which include: IM Olanzapine (Zyprexa), IM Aripiprazole (Abilify), or IM Ziprasidone (Geodon). Haldol is the most commonly
administered in hospital emergency settings due to availability. If Haldol is given, this is typically administered with Lorazepam (Ativan) along with Cogentin or Benadryl to avoid a potential EPS (Extrapyramidal Syndrome)

including dystonias. This can be given while using IM second generation antipsychotics, but not necessary.

Benzodiazepines can be used to treat agitation, but they are not required as most antipsychotics will treat agitation as well as psychosis.
