Case Summary
Differential: Hypopituitarism, Noonan syndrome.

The patient has Turner syndrome with coarctation of the aorta. Turner syndrome is a genetic abnormality (45,X genotype). Features include short stature, webbed neck, wide-spaced nipples, and shield chest. Coarctation of the aorta is common. Physical exam typically reveals a systolic murmur and differing systolic blood pressure between upper and lower extremities. Initial screening includes chest X-ray and ECG, with echocardiography as the gold standard for coarctation. Chromosome analysis (karyotype) confirms Turner syndrome. Management includes cardiothoracic surgery for coarctation and pediatric endocrinology for growth hormone therapy. Growth hormone levels are usually normal but not utilized correctly; estradiol replacement may be needed for ovarian failure.
