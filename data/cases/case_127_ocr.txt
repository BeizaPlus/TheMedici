Case Summary
Differential: Drug-induced immune thrombocytopenia, Liver Disease, Hereditary thrombocytopenia, Myelodysplastic Disorders
This patient in this case has Immune thrombocytopenia (ITP). The patient most likely developed ITP after having a recent viral infection. ITP patients will have platelet counts less than 100,000/microL with a normal white

blood cell count and hemoglobin. The majority of children develop ITP after a recent viral illness. The patient has gingival bleeding and petechiae. This would classify the patient as a grade III/IV between moderate and severe.
The patient will require treatment with either glucocorticoids, IVIG, or IV anti-D.

The patient is at risk for potentially severe bleeding if injured, so the patient should be counseled to avoid contact sports as well as to avoid antiplatelet medications like Aspirin and NSAIDs. Depending on the treatment

choice, the patient can be treated either inpatient or outpatient. Once the patient gets treatment, he will improve.
